How I Work

01
Brief & Creative Direction
Every project starts with a single question: what should this make the viewer feel? I translate brand objectives into a creative language — color, movement, atmosphere — before a single polygon is placed.
Moodboards Ps Ai
02
3D Modelling & Scene Assembly
Assets are modelled in Blender or Character Creator, textured in Substance, and assembled in Unreal Engine 5 where lighting happens in real time. No waiting on render farms.
Blender Character Creator 4 Substance 3D Painter Marvelous Designer
03
Lighting & Camera
UE5's Lumen gives me cinematic global illumination live. I direct the camera the way a DP would — considering focal length, depth of field, and the emotional weight of the shot.
Unreal Engine 5 Lumen GI
04
Compositing & VFX
Rendered sequences come into After Effects for compositing, colour work, and VFX layers. Motion blur, lens distortion, film grain — the details that make CGI feel physical.
After Effects Photoshop
05
Edit, Grade & Deliver
Final assembly and colour grade in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Sound design in Audition. Delivery optimised per platform — vertical for social, wide for broadcast.
Premiere Pro DaVinci Resolve Audition Lightroom
