World 04 — Environment

Helios

Vast solar architecture — a world built around and towards the light. Cold blue at the edges. Built in Unreal Engine 5.

Overview

Environment Design

Helios is defined by scale and directed light — structures oriented toward a distant sun, casting long shadows across an empty expanse. The palette is cool and cerulean, suggesting altitude and isolation.

1,000+ frames rendered in Unreal Engine 5 at 1920×1080. Camera movement follows the architecture's orientation — always moving toward the source.
