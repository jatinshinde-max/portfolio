Why realtime over pre-rendered?

Unreal Engine 5 gives me live feedback. I direct light the way a cinematographer would — standing in the scene, not waiting for a render farm.

02

CGI in service of story?

Every frame I produce answers one question: does this make you feel something? Technical perfection is the floor. Emotion is the ceiling.

03

What does "leading a team" mean to you?

I set the standard visually, then get out of the way. My designers and editors need to feel the work is theirs too — I'm a creative director, not a micromanager.
