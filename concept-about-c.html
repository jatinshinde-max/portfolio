Profile 01 / 04

CGI artist and VFX director with 7+ years of production experience across commercial campaigns, product visualisation, and cinematic environments.

I lead teams of graphic designers and video editors, translating complex creative briefs into finished visual work. My practice spans the full pipeline — from 3D modelling and character creation to realtime rendering in Unreal Engine 5, compositing in After Effects, and delivery in DaVinci Resolve.

The work I care about most merges technical execution with directorial intent — every commercial, every frame, every world is a crafted experience, not a rendered export.

Approach 04 / 04
  • Story first, technique second. I start every project by asking what the work needs to feel like, not what software I'll use to make it.
  • Realtime over batch rendering. Unreal Engine 5 lets me direct lighting and camera as a cinematographer would — iterating in seconds, not hours.
  • Team direction through visual standards. I set the quality bar with my own frames, then give my designers and editors the space to own their craft.
  • Full pipeline ownership. From brief to delivery, I manage the entire production — not as a specialist but as an end-to-end creative and technical director.
